About me

I am a geospatial researcher working on human-environment interactions, remote sensing, GIScience, vegetation and environmental change monitoring, and climate change. I hold an MSc in Cartography from Eötvös Loránd University (CGPA 4.94/5.00) and two degrees in Geography and Environment from Jahangirnagar University.

I currently work as a Geospatial Research Assistant at PlantVision, Hungary, building automated Python workflows for spectral data and vegetation monitoring. My methods span LULC classification, time-series analysis, UAV data, and deep-learning (CNN) mapping, and I have presented at the AGIT conference in Salzburg in 2025 and 2026.
